# Feature parity with UU Remote

The user, 2026-11-21: every function UU Remote has, Queqiao has too. This list comes from UU Remote 4.48.1's
interface text and its program and driver inventory (research notes kept outside this repository). It names what
each feature does, in our own words; nothing of UU's is copied. NetEase's own service business (cloud PCs, queues,
U-coin payments, memberships, real-name checks, the MuMu emulator) does not apply to Queqiao and is left out.

Status: **done**, **partly**, **planned** (with the milestone), **later** (after the Windows release).

| Codec choice: H.264, H.265, AV1 when both ends have hardware | AV1 done (2026-11-22): "Picture codec" in the session menu (Automatic, H.264, AV1); Automatic takes AV1 when the controlled PC encodes it on its GPU and the viewer decodes it on its GPU (Windows' AV1 Video Extension and Direct3D's AV1 profile), otherwise H.264; the switch happens in the session (a new encoder, a keyframe, the viewer's decoder follows the stream). Measured on the PC (RTX 5080): screen change -> shown 13.8 ms median, 17.3 p95, against 14.9 and 20.2 for H.264; `codec_probe codec=av1` round trip PASS (PSNR >= 40.8 dB); `run_m4_local.ps1` runs most sessions in AV1 and one in H.264. H.265 in Automatic (2026-11-26, the user: H.265 left out was "ridiculous"): Automatic takes the best codec both ends do on their GPUs, AV1, then H.265, then H.264 (the agent opens each encoder once to know; the viewer reports only H.265 its GPU decodes; the phone apps ask their system for a hardware H.265 decoder); 4:4:4 and 10-bit stay separate switches, off by default, applied when the picture is H.265. Measured on sunshine's Iris Xe (it makes no AV1; `codec_probe`, 2240x1400 at 5 Mbit/s, the page moving 7/28/56 rows a frame, luma PSNR): H.264 38.5/40.5/39.6 dB, H.265 48.2/48.9/41.0 on Intel's balanced preset (on the fastest one, 47.8/37.1/30.7: its motion search fell short of a quick drag, so H.265 on oneVPL uses preset 4); screen change -> sent in a session over its LTE modem 14.6 ms median against 14.1 for H.264 |
